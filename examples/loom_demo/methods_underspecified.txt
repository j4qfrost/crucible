Demo Methods text — under-specified variant (Shaw et al. 2010 BPTI, abstract-only).
=================================================================================

Source attribution
------------------
This is a deliberately under-specified Methods paragraph derived ONLY from the
PUBLIC abstract of:

  D. E. Shaw, P. Maragakis, K. Lindorff-Larsen, S. Piana, R. O. Dror,
  M. P. Eastwood, J. A. Bank, J. M. Jumper, J. K. Salmon, Y. Shan, W. Wriggers.
  "Atomic-Level Characterization of the Structural Dynamics of Proteins."
  Science 330, 341–346 (2010). DOI: 10.1126/science.1187409.

The full Methods are in the Supplementary Information, which is paywalled and
has not been read for this file. As a result this paragraph deliberately
omits the protein force field, water model, integrator timestep, temperature,
ensemble, and pressure — exactly the kind of under-specification the LLM
transpile path encounters with real-world papers.

Why this exists
---------------
The companion `methods.txt` file in this directory is a synthetic full-detail
Methods paragraph (Abl+imatinib FEP) that exercises the happy path. This
under-specified variant is what real MD methods sections often look like and
shows the transpiler's behavior when the LLM has to flag missing protocol
fields. Cross-reference: docs/HALLUCINATION_BENCHMARK.md (entry
`shaw_2010_bpti`).

Methods (paraphrased from the Shaw 2010 abstract)
-------------------------------------------------
We performed an atomic-level all-atom molecular dynamics simulation of bovine
pancreatic trypsin inhibitor (BPTI) in explicit solvent on a special-purpose
machine. The simulation duration was 1 millisecond — the longest single
trajectory of a folded protein reported at the time. The simulation revealed
multiple distinct long-lived conformational states of BPTI; transitions
between these states occurred on timescales roughly three orders of magnitude
slower than local atomic relaxations.
