Demo Methods text (for the Loom recording — swap with a real paper before publication)
=======================================================================================

The system was prepared from PDB 2HYY (Abelson tyrosine kinase in complex with imatinib).
Hydrogens were added at pH 7.4 using PDBFixer; missing side chains were modeled with
default rotamers. The complex was solvated in a cubic box of TIP3P water with a 12 Å
buffer between the solute and the periodic box edge, and neutralized with 150 mM NaCl
(38 Na+ / 41 Cl- counter-ions). Final system size was approximately 45,000 atoms.

Force-field parameters were assigned from AMBER ff14SB for the protein and Open Force
Field 2.2.0 ("Sage") for the imatinib ligand, generated with the OpenFF Toolkit.

Energy minimization (steepest descent, 2,000 iterations, 10 kJ/mol/nm tolerance) was
followed by NVT equilibration at 310 K for 500 ps with heavy-atom positional restraints
(2,000 kJ/mol/nm^2). NPT equilibration at 310 K and 1 bar was performed for 2 ns with
backbone restraints (500 kJ/mol/nm^2) using a Monte Carlo barostat. The production
ensemble was NPT at 310 K and 1 bar, integrated with the Langevin middle integrator at
1 ps^-1 friction and a 2 fs timestep, with hydrogen bonds constrained via SHAKE.

Binding free energy was estimated by alchemical decoupling FEP across 11 lambda windows
(0.0, 0.1, 0.2, ..., 1.0) of 2 ns each, with samples written every 10 ps. Trajectories
were recorded every 20 ps for the production phase. RMSD of the protein C-alpha atoms
was tracked at 20 ps stride for monitoring conformational stability.

OpenMM 8.5 was used as the simulation engine, running on a single A100 GPU at mixed
precision.
